# Predictions for the weaker-model test

Written before any weaker-model run. Joshua said yes to the test on 3 October 2026, about 00:40 UTC. Claude designed the test and wrote these predictions; Joshua directs the work. Do not edit this file; its fingerprint is sealed with OpenTimestamps.

## Why this test

The same-model comparison was a tie. GPT-6.1 at high effort got all 48 records right from the raw logs and through Sanity Context (results/model-pair-2026-10-02T23-55-00-823Z). A strong model did not need the structure. This test asks whether the structure helps models that do make false "done" on raw logs.

## Design

- **Models, through the owner's broker on OpenRouter:**
  - `google/gemini-3.7-flash`;
  - `openai/gpt-5.4-nano`.

  The published benchmark (It Quoted the Failure) recorded false "done" for both under a plain report prompt:
  - Gemini 3.7 Flash on failed checks, in 7 of 16 scenarios over three runs;
  - GPT-5.4 nano on checks that never ran, in 6 of 16.
- **Arms:** raw logs, and Sanity Context. Both use the same frozen prompts, scorer and 48 records as the GPT-6.1 comparison.
- **Runs:** one run per model per arm, with a fresh session for each record, no tools, and the provider's default sampling.
- **Measures, from the unchanged scorer:**
  - false "done" on failed checks (of 16);
  - false "done" on checks that never ran (of 16);
  - correct status (of 48);
  - receipt score.
- **Rules:**
  - A missing or invalid reply counts as wrong.
  - Nothing is re-run selectively.
  - If a model fails to answer more than 4 of 48 records in an arm, that arm is reported as incomplete.
  - Every reply is kept.
- **Spend cap:** 2 USD for the whole test. Stop at the cap, or when the broker says the credit is gone.

## Predictions

- **P1.** Gemini 3.7 Flash, raw logs: at least 2 false "done" on failed checks.
- **P2.** GPT-5.4 nano, raw logs: at least 2 false "done" on checks that never ran.
- **P3.** For each model whose raw arm has at least 2 false "done" (both kinds together), the Context arm has at most half as many.
- **P4.** For each model, correct status through Context is at least as high as from the raw logs.
- **P5.** For each model, the receipt score through Context is at least as high as from the raw logs.

## Reporting

Each prediction is scored hit or miss, with misses beside hits. The GPT-6.1 tie stays the headline result whatever this test shows. This test is reported as a separate, pre-registered addition.
